## Regex Blocklist
## regular expressions that cover groups of subdomains with the same prefix/sufix 
## [260403] Initial release


## Google messager (hangouts)
/alt[1-9][0-9]?-mtalk\.google\.com/


## Block domains and subdomains related to Google AMP (Accelerated Mobile Pages)It identifies the term "amp" (optionally followed by "project")
/^(.+[_.-])?amp(project)?\./

## Block all domains with "analytics"
/^analytics?[-.]/
